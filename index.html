Straight answers

Before we start.

No inflated promises. Just the useful details businesses usually want to know first.

What does Qixton help businesses with?+

Qixton connects web design, SEO, social media, paid ads, AEO/GEO and reputation support around one clear growth goal.

Will my website be custom-built?+

Yes. The structure, copy and visual system are shaped around your business rather than copied from a generic industry template.

How quickly will I see results?+

Timelines depend on the service, starting position and competition. Qixton will set realistic milestones after reviewing your situation.

How are website projects paid for?+

Website projects are normally split into milestones—typically 50% to begin and the remaining 50% at completion, confirmed in your proposal.

Do you work with international clients?+

Yes. Qixton is based in India and is designed to work remotely with ambitious businesses in international markets.
